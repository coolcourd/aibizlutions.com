Why We Built Bo Botty

AI Bizlutions was founded to solve a problem we kept seeing firsthand: businesses invest in powerful tools that promise efficiency, but those tools are complex to integrate, difficult to fully utilize, and often fail to deliver sustained value once deployed.

Across industries, teams are stretched thin — juggling fragmented systems, manual processes, and disconnected technology while trying to manage growth. AI and automation should help, but without proper integration and ongoing management, they quickly become shelf-ware.

That's why we built Bo Botty, Your AI Business Partner.

Chase's Story

Chase Headman's background was built in high-stakes aviation operations, where precision, accountability, and execution are non-negotiable.

Starting his career as an aircraft mechanic, Chase progressed through maintenance leadership, quality systems, and operational management roles before ultimately becoming the Accountable Manager and Head of Fleet Operations for an international aviation organization overseeing more than 100 aircraft.

In that environment, systems are expected to perform flawlessly. Processes must be repeatable, decisions must withstand scrutiny, and operational failures carry serious consequences. Those experiences shaped Chase's philosophy around leadership, automation, and operational infrastructure.

Beyond safety and compliance, aviation also demands relentless efficiency. Margins are tight, coordination is complex, and even small breakdowns in communication or workflow can create major operational impacts. Chase developed a deep focus on process improvement, risk mitigation, and building systems that reduce friction while improving consistency and reliability.

That operational mindset eventually expanded into business analytics, strategic planning, and technology-driven process optimization, including extensive work with platforms such as Microsoft Power BI to support executive decision-making and organizational performance.

While working across multiple industries, Chase noticed a recurring issue: many small businesses were overwhelmed by disconnected software, inconsistent follow-up, missed opportunities, and time-consuming manual processes. Meanwhile, enterprise organizations were using automation and AI to streamline operations and scale more effectively.

Recognizing that most smaller companies lacked the internal resources to build those systems themselves, Chase co-founded AI Bizlutions to help close that gap.

Today, he helps businesses implement intelligent operational systems that improve customer responsiveness, automate repetitive tasks, strengthen internal workflows, and create scalable processes designed for long-term growth.

Together with his brother Courd, Chase helped develop "Bo Botty - Your AI Business Partner," combining operational discipline with modern AI technology to bring enterprise-level capabilities to growing businesses without enterprise-level complexity.

For Chase, successful automation is not about removing people from business - it is about freeing people to focus on higher-value work while systems handle the operational load behind the scenes.

Courd's Story

Courd Headman's passion for technology started long before his professional career began.

As a kid, he immersed himself in computers, teaching himself skills in video editing, 3D design, Linux systems, and programming simply because he enjoyed understanding how technology worked and how it could be improved.

That curiosity eventually turned into a software engineering career spanning more than a decade, where Courd specialized in designing automation systems, integrating platforms, and developing scalable technical solutions that improve efficiency and eliminate unnecessary manual work.

Over the years, he built a reputation for creating systems that dramatically increased productivity by simplifying complex workflows and automating repetitive tasks. Rather than accepting inefficient processes, Courd consistently looked for ways to engineer smarter solutions that saved time, reduced friction, and improved execution.

As automation and AI technologies evolved, he saw a major imbalance in the marketplace. Large corporations were rapidly gaining advantages through sophisticated automation systems and dedicated technical teams, while many small and mid-sized businesses were left trying to compete using outdated or disconnected processes.

That realization became one of the driving forces behind the creation of AI Bizlutions.

Alongside his brother Chase, Courd set out to make advanced automation and AI technology accessible to businesses that traditionally would not have access to enterprise-grade systems or in-house development teams.

Today, Courd leads the technical development and systems architecture behind AI Bizlutions, helping businesses modernize operations through workflow automation, AI-powered customer interaction, streamlined communication systems, and intelligent integrations.

His approach is rooted in a simple belief: technology should remove obstacles, not create them.

By combining powerful automation with practical implementation, Courd helps businesses operate more efficiently, respond faster, and compete at a level that was once only possible for major corporations.
