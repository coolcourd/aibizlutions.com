Why we started AI Bizlutions

If you own a small business, you already know the feeling. You keep your customers in a notebook and a head full of names. You chase invoices at night. You can tell a good month from a bad one but not always why. The social post you meant to write is still sitting in your head. And every year, no matter how hard you work, the margins get a little thinner.

The problem is your tools, not you. The chain that opened down the road catches every lead in a CRM and follows up every time, without a single person remembering to. Its brand is the same colors, the same voice, the same look on the storefront, the website, the invoice, and the ad, so by the third time someone sees it they feel like they already know it. That recognition is half the reason people choose the chain over you. Its managers know exactly which products, which days, and which customers are making them money, and they know it before you have found your notebook. Its social channels post polished content every single day. They spent decades and millions building those systems to win your customers.

You are doing it all by hand while the other side brought the right tools. Small businesses are the heart of every community we have ever lived in, and right now they are being put out of business, on purpose, by companies with budgets they cannot imagine.

That imbalance is the whole reason AI Bizlutions exists.

So we take the systems the big players use to win and put them in your hands, built around your business by our team or ready to go in our own product, Bo Botty. A real CRM, so no lead or customer ever slips through. A brand that is recognizably yours, the same everywhere a customer meets you, because people buy from names they recognize, and right now the chain is the name they recognize. Metrics that show you where the money is really coming from and where it is quietly leaking out. Social media that keeps going out every day, planned ahead so a busy week doesn't stop it. Invoicing, scheduling, and the thousand small tasks that eat your evenings. And underneath all of it, the same AI the big players use, doing the work that used to take a department. All at a price you can actually pay, with no IT team and no six-month rollout.

We don't want to turn you into a corporation. We want you to have a fair fight against the ones that want you gone. The personal touch, the local knowledge, the care a chain can never fake: that is already yours. We just make sure you have the same tools they do.

Margins get thinner when one side has the systems and the other doesn't. We're here to even that out.

Chase's story

Chase started out as an aircraft mechanic. Over the years he moved into maintenance leadership, quality systems and operations management, and eventually became Accountable Manager and Head of Fleet Operations for an international aviation organization with more than 100 aircraft.

In aviation, a skipped step can ground an aircraft. Work has to be done the same way every time, decisions have to hold up when someone checks them, and the margins are thin. That is where Chase learned to build systems that keep a busy operation running without relying on anyone's memory.

Later he moved into business analytics and planning, building Microsoft Power BI reports that showed leaders what was working and what wasn't.

Working across several industries, he kept seeing the same thing: small businesses buried in apps that don't talk to each other, follow-ups that slipped, and late nights of manual work, while the chains competing with them had connected systems that let them answer faster, market harder and live on thinner margins.

So he founded AI Bizlutions to give small businesses the same tools, not a watered-down version, and later built Bo Botty to put many of them in one app.

His view on automation is simple: it should take the repetitive work off people's plates so they have more time for their customers, not replace them.
