What Bo Botty Does For You

One simple app and one AI helper for your marketing, your leads and your paperwork, so you can get back to running your business.

Marketing that runs itself.

Bo Botty learns your business from your website, or from a few sentences if you're just starting out, and builds your brand. Then it writes a week of social posts, pictures included, ready for you to approve. Bo helps you answer the comments that come in.

  • Builds your brand look and voice for you
  • Plans your posts and publishes them once you approve
  • Helps you reply to comments on your posts

Every lead, followed up.

Leads from your website forms land straight in your contact list, along with where they came from. Bo Botty drafts the follow-up emails, sends them with your OK, and keeps every conversation in one place, so nobody falls through the cracks.

  • Website forms that fill your contact list
  • Sends invoices, replies and campaigns from your own Gmail or Outlook account
  • Every customer's history in one place

Just tell Bo what you need.

Type or say it in plain words: send a quote, invoice a customer, post this week's special. Bo Botty does the work and asks you first before anything goes out. No training, no setup manual.

  • Quotes and invoices customers pay online
  • Ask in plain English, like texting an assistant
  • Asks before anything goes out, until you say otherwise

Why We Built Bo Botty

If you own a small business, you already know the feeling. You keep your customers in a notebook and a head full of names. You chase invoices at night. You can tell a good month from a bad one but not always why. The social post you meant to write is still sitting in your head. And every year, no matter how hard you work, the margins get a little thinner.

That is not a you problem. It is a systems problem. The chain that opened down the road catches every lead in a CRM and follows up every time, without a single person remembering to. Its brand is the same colors, the same voice, the same look on the storefront, the website, the invoice, and the ad, so by the third time someone sees it they feel like they already know it. That recognition is half the reason people choose the chain over you, and it did not happen by accident. Its managers know exactly which products, which days, and which customers are making them money, and they know it before you have found your notebook. Its social channels post polished content every single day. None of that is magic. It is systems, and they spent decades and millions building them so they could take your customers.

You are doing it all by hand while the other side brought the right tools. Small businesses are the heart of every community we have ever lived in, and right now they are being put out of business, on purpose, by companies with budgets they cannot imagine.

That imbalance is the whole reason AI Bizlutions exists.

We built Bo Botty, Your AI Business Partner, to take the systems the big players use to win and put them in your hands. A real CRM, so no lead or customer ever slips through. A brand that is recognizably yours, the same everywhere a customer meets you, because people buy from names they recognize, and right now the chain is the name they recognize. Metrics that show you where the money is really coming from and where it is quietly leaking out. Social media that keeps going out every day, planned ahead so a busy week doesn't stop it. Invoicing, scheduling, and the thousand small tasks that eat your evenings. And underneath all of it, the same AI the big players use, doing the work that used to take a department. All at a price you can actually pay, with no IT team and no six-month rollout.

We are not trying to turn you into a corporation. We are trying to give you a fighting chance against the ones that want you gone. The personal touch, the local knowledge, the care a chain can never fake: that is already yours. Bo Botty just makes sure you have the same tools they do.

Ever tighter margins are not a law of nature. They are what happens when one side has the systems and the other does not. We are here to even the fight.

Chase's Story

Chase Headman's background was built in high-stakes aviation operations, where precision, accountability, and execution are non-negotiable.

Starting his career as an aircraft mechanic, Chase progressed through maintenance leadership, quality systems, and operational management roles before ultimately becoming the Accountable Manager and Head of Fleet Operations for an international aviation organization overseeing more than 100 aircraft.

In that environment, systems are expected to perform flawlessly. Processes must be repeatable, decisions must withstand scrutiny, and operational failures carry serious consequences. Those experiences shaped Chase's philosophy around leadership, automation, and operational infrastructure.

Beyond safety and compliance, aviation also demands relentless efficiency. Margins are tight, coordination is complex, and even small breakdowns in communication or workflow can create major operational impacts. Chase developed a deep focus on process improvement, risk mitigation, and building systems that reduce friction while improving consistency and reliability.

That operational mindset eventually expanded into business analytics, strategic planning, and technology-driven process optimization, including extensive work with platforms such as Microsoft Power BI to support executive decision-making and organizational performance.

While working across multiple industries, Chase kept seeing the same thing: small businesses drowning in disconnected software, missed follow-ups, and late nights of manual work, while the corporations competing against them ran on connected systems, a CRM, a brand people recognized, a clear read on the numbers, a marketing machine, that let them respond faster, market harder, and run on margins no local shop could match. The big players were not better at business. They had better tools.

Convinced that small businesses deserved the same tools, not a watered-down version of them, Chase founded AI Bizlutions to put them within reach of the companies being squeezed without them.

Today, he helps businesses implement intelligent operational systems that improve customer responsiveness, automate repetitive tasks, strengthen internal workflows, and create scalable processes designed for long-term growth.

Chase went on to build "Bo Botty - Your AI Business Partner," combining operational discipline with modern AI technology to bring enterprise-level capabilities to growing businesses without enterprise-level complexity.

For Chase, successful automation is not about removing people from business - it is about freeing people to focus on higher-value work while systems handle the operational load behind the scenes.
