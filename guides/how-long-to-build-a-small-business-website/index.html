How long does it take to build a small business website?

By AI Bizlutions. Published November 22, 2026.

A simple small business website typically takes anywhere from a few weekends if you build it yourself, to about 2 to 8 weeks with a freelancer, to 2 to 4 months with an agency. With us, you see three free designs in minutes, and we aim for a first look at your tailored site about a week after your kick-off call. If it will take longer, we tell you.

Building it yourself

With a do-it-yourself website builder, you can have something online in a day. A site you are proud of usually takes longer: writing every page, picking and resizing photos, setting up your domain and checking it on a phone. Most owners fit this around their business, so a few weekends or a few weeks of evenings is common.

Working with a freelancer

A freelancer often needs about 2 to 8 weeks for a small site. Much of that time is waiting: for a slot in their schedule, for your words and photos, and for your feedback on each round. Ask for a schedule with dates before you start.

Working with an agency

Agencies usually take about 2 to 4 months, sometimes more. They plan, write, design and test in stages, with meetings and sign-offs between each one. That process brings polish, and it also takes time.

What slows a website down

  • Waiting on words and photos. This is the most common delay on every route. Gather them early.
  • Too many people deciding. Pick one person to give feedback and approve changes.
  • Changing the plan halfway. New pages or features in the middle restart part of the work.
  • Domain and login access. Find out who controls your web address before you start.

How to get your site live faster

  • Make sure your Google listing is complete and current, with good photos and your real hours
  • Write down your main services or menu, and a few lines about your business
  • Pick your favorite three or four customer reviews
  • Set aside time to reply to feedback within a day or two

Common questions

Can I get a website in one day?

You can get a basic page online in a day with a builder. A site that is clear, accurate and works well on a phone usually takes more care than that. With us, three designs for your business are usually ready a few minutes after you ask.

What do I need ready before I start?

Your business name, address, phone number, hours, a list of your services or menu, a few photos and a few reviews. If your Google listing has these, you already have most of it.

Can I update my website after it goes live?

Yes. Hours change, prices change and new photos come in. Make sure you know who makes those changes and how quickly.
